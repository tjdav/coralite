Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA

Our commitment to making coralite.dev usable with keyboards, screen readers and other assistive technologies.

Last updated 18 November 2026

Commitment

coralite.dev aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA. Accessibility is treated as part of the documentation work, not as a separate project.

Conformance status

This statement is a self-assessment. The site has not yet been independently audited or tested with the full range of assistive technologies, so we do not claim full conformance. We describe the site as aiming to conform to WCAG 2.2 AA.

What we do

  • Semantic HTML landmarks and heading order on every page.
  • Keyboard access to navigation, search and interactive components, with visible focus states.
  • Colour combinations chosen for contrast against the dark theme.
  • Text alternatives for informative images and icons; decorative elements are hidden from assistive technology.
  • Content that works without client-side JavaScript. Search is the only feature that requires it.

Known limitations

  • The site has not been audited with screen readers or switch devices, so edge cases may remain.
  • Complex interactive widgets, such as the command palette, mobile navigation and tooltips, may be harder to operate with some assistive technologies.
  • Older blog posts may contain code samples or tables that are harder to follow on narrow screens.
  • External sites we link to are outside our control.

Feedback

If you encounter a barrier, write to tom@mightysmile.com with the page address and a short description of the problem. We will respond as soon as possible and fix what we can.

Complaints

This is a private website and does not fall within the scope of Spanish Royal Decree 1112/2018 on accessibility of public sector websites. There is therefore no formal enforcement procedure, but we still welcome reports and take them seriously.

Review

This statement is reviewed at least once a year and whenever the site's structure or components change significantly.

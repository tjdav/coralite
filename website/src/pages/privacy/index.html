Privacy Policy

What happens to your data when you visit coralite.dev, and the rights you have over it.

Last updated 18 November 2026

In short: no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only personal data involved is the IP address your browser needs to receive the page, and any message you choose to send us.

Who is responsible

Thomas David (autónomo), Barcelona, Spain. Email: tom@mightysmile.com. The full operator details, including the tax identification number and address, are in the Legal Notice.

Hosting and server data

The site is hosted by statichost.eu (Variable Object Assignment, Stockholm, Sweden), within the European Economic Area. To deliver a page, the hosting infrastructure must process the IP address of the visitor making the request. According to statichost.eu's privacy policy, it does not store visit data, does not use third-party processors for website traffic, and does not transfer data outside the EEA. It also collects anonymous usage statistics that do not identify individual visitors.

Our legal basis for using this hosting is our legitimate interest in publishing the site (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Messages you send us

If you email us, we process your email address and the content of your message in order to reply. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to you (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), or your consent when you initiate the contact (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). We keep correspondence for up to 12 months after the request is resolved, then delete it, unless we are required to keep it longer by law.

Cookies and local storage

This site does not set cookies and does not store data in your browser. The search index is downloaded as a static file and your queries are processed entirely in your browser; they are never sent to a server. There are no analytics scripts, advertising pixels or third-party embeds.

Third-party links

Some pages link to external sites such as GitHub, npm or statichost.eu. Once you follow a link, the privacy policy of that site applies. We do not receive data about your visit to those sites.

Recipients and international transfers

The only recipient of personal data is our hosting provider, acting as a processor under a data processing agreement. Data is processed within the European Economic Area. We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone else.

Publishing operator details

We publish the operator's name, NIF and address on the Legal Notice because Spanish Law 34/2002 requires it. The legal basis for that publication is compliance with a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

Your rights

You can ask us to access, rectify, erase or restrict the processing of your personal data, object to processing, request portability, or withdraw consent at any time. Write to tom@mightysmile.com and we will respond as soon as possible.

If you are not satisfied, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish data protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD).

Children

The site is technical documentation and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If the site starts using a new service or changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated and the date above revised.
